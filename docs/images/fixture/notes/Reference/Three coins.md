# The three-coin method

Three coins, six times. Each throw gives one line, counted from the bottom up.

| Coins | Value | Line | Changing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 tails | 6 | broken | yes |
| 2 tails, 1 head | 7 | solid | no |
| 1 tail, 2 heads | 8 | broken | no |
| 3 heads | 9 | solid | yes |

A changing line turns into its opposite, which produces a second hexagram —
the situation the first one is moving towards.
